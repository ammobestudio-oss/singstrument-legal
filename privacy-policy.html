Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

SingStrument ("we", "our", or "the app") provides real-time voice-to-instrument synthesis and audio conversion tools. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Microphone & Audio Processing

SingStrument requires access to your device's microphone strictly to perform real-time pitch detection, note quantization, and instrument synthesis. All audio signal processing occurs entirely on your device in real time. We do not record, store, transmit, or sell your raw voice data to external servers.

3. User Files & Custom SoundFonts (.sf2)

Any audio recordings you save, exported MIDI/WAV files, or imported custom SoundFont (.sf2) files are stored exclusively in your device's private local sandbox storage. These files remain entirely private to you and are never uploaded to any shared network or server.

4. Permissions Used

  • RECORD_AUDIO: Required for live vocal pitch estimation and synthesizer triggering.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used solely to allow you to export songs to your public Music/Downloads folder and import local .sf2 files.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use standard platform services (such as Google Play Services and Firebase) for crash reporting, core performance analytics, and dynamic asset delivery. These services do not collect identifiable personal audio recordings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@singstrument.app
