Privacy at a Glance

SingStrument is an on-device music creation and transcription application.

Your microphone recordings, imported songs, generated melodies, musical notes, and music exports are processed locally on your device.

SingStrument does not upload these personal music files to our Firebase backend during recording, transcription, instrument synthesis, or audio export operations.

Account authentication, official instrument downloads, security verification, and selected online services use Google Firebase and Google Play services.

We do not sell your personal information or personal audio recordings.

1. Overview

SingStrument ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") is developed and operated by Ammobe Studio.

The app provides voice-to-note transcription, musical pitch detection, Indian Sargam and Western note generation, instrument playback, audio recording, music arrangements, custom note input, and MIDI/WAV export.

This Privacy Policy explains what information SingStrument accesses, processes, stores, transmits, and deletes.

For privacy-related questions, contact: singlovestrument@gmail.com .

2. Audio, Voice and Music Processing

SingStrument may request microphone access when you choose to record singing, humming, or other audio.

2.1 Microphone Recording

Audio recorded through the microphone is processed locally for pitch analysis, transcription, playback, and music creation.

Microphone recordings are not uploaded to our Firebase backend as part of these recording and processing features.

2.2 Imported Audio Files

You may select supported audio files, including formats such as MP3, WAV, M4A, and AAC, for music analysis.

These files are decoded and processed locally on your device.

2.3 AI Pitch and Melody Detection

SingStrument uses local audio-processing and machine-learning technologies, which may include:

  • YIN pitch detection.
  • CREPE-related pitch analysis.
  • RMVPE neural pitch estimation.
  • ONNX Runtime.
  • Melody and note post-processing.

These processing components run on the user's device for supported workflows. SingStrument does not send personal recordings to Firebase for pitch inference.

2.4 Generated Music and Exports

Musical notes, Sargam, MIDI data, instrumental arrangements, and generated audio are processed locally.

When you export or share a file, it may become accessible to another application or service selected by you. Such external services are governed by their own privacy practices.

3. Local Data Storage

SingStrument may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Voice and microphone recordings.
  • Imported songs and audio files.
  • Generated musical notes and Sargam.
  • Saved melodies and music projects.
  • Custom note sequences.
  • MIDI files.
  • Generated WAV and other audio exports.
  • Downloaded SoundFont instruments.
  • User-imported SoundFont (.sf2) files.
  • Temporary processing files and caches.

Personal music files are not stored in our Firebase Firestore database or Firebase Storage as part of the normal music-processing workflow.

Files explicitly exported to public locations, such as Music/SingStrument or Downloads, may remain on your device until you delete them.

Clearing app data or uninstalling SingStrument may remove app-private content, but may not remove files exported to public device storage.

4. Account Information

SingStrument uses Firebase Authentication and supported Google Sign-In services to manage user accounts.

Depending on your authentication method, we may process:

  • Firebase User ID (UID).
  • Email address.
  • Display name.
  • Profile photo URL.
  • Account role and status.
  • Account creation timestamp.
  • Last sign-in information.
  • Authentication and security metadata.

This information is used to provide authentication, account management, access control, security, and supported online features.

Google manages your Google Account authentication credentials. We do not ask users to provide their Google Account passwords directly to our support team.

5. Firebase and Cloud Services

SingStrument uses selected Google Firebase services for account-related and online application functionality.

5.1 Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication manages user authentication and identity.

It may automatically process technical information such as IP addresses, user-agent information, application identifiers, and authentication request metadata for service operation and security.

5.2 Cloud Firestore

Cloud Firestore may store:

  • User profile metadata.
  • Account status and role information.
  • User instrument requests.
  • Official instrument metadata.
  • Application configuration.
  • Administrator and security records.

Cloud Firestore is not used as a storage destination for personal audio recordings or generated musical exports.

5.3 Firebase Storage

Firebase Storage may host official application resources, including downloadable SoundFont instruments and related resource files.

These files are downloaded to the user's device when requested.

User-imported songs, personal recordings, and generated music are not uploaded to this official instrument resource system.

5.4 Cloud Functions

Cloud Functions may support protected account deletion, administrative operations, instrument management, and security-related workflows.

5.5 Firebase App Check and Play Integrity

Where enabled, Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity help protect online resources against unauthorized access and automated abuse.

These services may process app identifiers, integrity tokens, device or application metadata, and other information necessary for security verification.

6. Official Instrument Downloads

SingStrument provides downloadable musical instruments that may include piano, guitar, bansuri, harmonium, saxophone, and other SoundFont-based instruments.

Official instrument files may be downloaded from Firebase Storage through authenticated and protected network requests.

Downloaded instruments may be cached locally for repeated use.

Requests to online services may include account, network, and security information required to deliver the requested files.

Personal SoundFonts imported by users are not automatically uploaded to the official instrument library.

7. Instrument Requests and Support

If you request a new instrument through SingStrument, the app may store the request and associated account information.

Such information may include your account identifier, instrument request details, and submission time.

If you contact our support team, we may process your email address and the information you choose to include in your message.

Support information is used to respond to requests, resolve problems, and manage account-related inquiries.

8. Data Sharing and Advertising

Ammobe Studio does not sell personal information or personal audio recordings.

Google Firebase and Google Play services process relevant information as service providers for the functions described in this policy.

SingStrument does not currently use personal audio recordings for targeted advertising.

If our advertising, analytics, or data-sharing practices change, we will update our disclosures as required.

9. Android Permissions

Depending on the Android version, supported features, and device configuration, SingStrument may use the following permissions:

9.1 Microphone — RECORD_AUDIO

Used when you choose to record singing, humming, or other audio.

Microphone access supports recording, pitch detection, and related audio-processing features.

The app requests microphone authorization through Android's permission system.

9.2 Audio Files — READ_MEDIA_AUDIO

On Android 13 and newer, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO may be used when the app requires access to audio files in the device's shared media library.

This supports user-initiated audio selection and import for music transcription.

SingStrument may also use Android's system file picker, which provides access to files explicitly selected by the user.

9.3 Legacy File Permissions

On older Android versions, legacy storage permissions may be used where required for compatible file access.

Modern Android versions use supported file-selection and media-saving APIs rather than unrestricted storage access.

9.4 Foreground Service — FOREGROUND_SERVICE

On supported builds, Android foreground-service functionality may be used for active, user-initiated operations that need to continue running.

When an applicable foreground service is active, Android may display a system notification.

This permission does not independently authorize microphone recording.

9.5 Audio Settings — MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS

Used where necessary to support audio playback configuration and instrument sound output.

9.6 Internet — INTERNET

Used for Google Sign-In, Firebase authentication, online security verification, official instrument downloads, and other supported online services.

You can manage runtime permissions through Android device settings. Disabling a required permission may prevent the related feature from functioning.
