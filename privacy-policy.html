Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Overview

SingStrument ("we", "our", "us", or "the app") provides voice-to-note transcription, melody detection, musical instrument playback, audio recording, MIDI/WAV export, and related music tools.

We are committed to protecting user privacy. Audio recordings, imported songs, generated notes, MIDI files, exported audio, and user-imported SoundFont files are designed to remain on the user's device and are not uploaded to our Firebase database or Firebase Storage.

2. Audio, Voice & Music Processing

SingStrument may request microphone access so that users can sing, hum, record audio, detect musical pitch, generate musical notes, and control instrument playback.

Voice-to-note and melody analysis, including pitch detection and the app's on-device transcription processing, is performed locally on the user's device.

We do not upload users' microphone recordings, imported songs, generated melodies, MIDI data, or raw voice recordings to Firebase.

3. Local Recordings, Notes & Exported Files

The following types of user-generated content are stored locally on the user's device:

  • Voice and song recordings
  • Imported songs and audio files
  • Detected musical notes and Sargam
  • MIDI files
  • Generated WAV/audio files
  • Melody and transcription results
  • User-imported custom SoundFont (.sf2) files

These files are not uploaded to our Firebase Firestore database or Firebase Storage.

Files that you explicitly export to locations such as your device's Music, Downloads, or other public folders remain on your device until you remove them.

4. Custom SoundFonts (.sf2)

Users may import their own SoundFont (.sf2) files for local instrument playback.

User-imported SoundFonts remain stored locally on the user's device. They are not uploaded to Firebase, shared with administrators, or made available to other users.

5. Account Information

SingStrument uses Firebase Authentication to provide user accounts and secure access to app services.

Depending on the authentication method used, we may receive and store account information such as:

  • Firebase user ID (UID)
  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile photo URL, if provided by the authentication provider
  • Account role, such as user or administrator
  • Account status
  • Account creation and last-login information

This information is used for authentication, account management, security, administrator access control, and operation of the app.

6. Firebase Services

SingStrument uses Firebase services provided by Google for limited backend functionality.

These services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication for user sign-in and account identity.
  • Cloud Firestore for account/profile metadata, administrator-related information, official instrument metadata, application configuration, and security/audit records.
  • Firebase Storage for official app-managed instrument SoundFont files that users may download after signing in.
  • Cloud Functions for Firebase for secure administrative actions, account deletion, administrator role management, and protected app configuration changes.
  • Firebase App Check / Google Play Integrity where enabled to help protect Firebase services against unauthorized or abusive access.

Firebase is not used to store users' personal song recordings, imported songs, transcription notes, MIDI exports, generated audio, or personal SoundFont files.

7. Official Instrument Downloads

SingStrument may provide official instruments such as guitar, piano, flute, harmonium, or other SoundFont-based instruments.

Official instrument files may be hosted using Firebase Storage and downloaded to the user's device when selected.

Downloaded official instruments may be cached locally so they can be used again without downloading them every time.

User-created or user-imported SoundFonts are not uploaded to this official instrument system.

8. Administrator Access

Authorized SingStrument administrators may access limited account and operational information required to manage the service.

This may include:

  • User UID
  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Account status
  • User or administrator role
  • Account-related timestamps

Administrators may also manage official instruments, application defaults, administrator permissions, and security audit information.

Administrators do not have access through Firebase to users' locally stored recordings, imported songs, generated notes, MIDI files, exported audio, or personal SoundFont files.

9. Permissions Used

Depending on the device and features being used, SingStrument may request permissions including:

  • Microphone / RECORD_AUDIO: Used for recording, singing/humming input, pitch detection, melody transcription, and live instrument functionality.
  • Media / File Access: Used when the user chooses to import an audio file or SoundFont, or export generated MIDI/audio files.
  • Internet Access: Used for account authentication, Firebase communication, official instrument downloads, administrator functionality, and application configuration.

Permissions are used only for the features that require them.

10. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their SingStrument account through the app's account deletion feature.

When an account deletion request is successfully completed, the app's backend deletes the associated Firebase Authentication account and the user's cloud account/profile information, subject to information that may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or legitimate audit purposes.

Because recordings, songs, notes, MIDI files, generated audio, and personal SoundFonts are stored locally rather than in Firebase, cloud account deletion does not represent deletion of files that the user has separately exported to public device storage.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards designed to protect account and application data.

These safeguards may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Role-based access controls
  • Firebase Security Rules
  • Protected administrator Cloud Functions
  • Firebase App Check / Play Integrity
  • Restricted access to official instrument files
  • Administrative audit logging

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Selling & Advertising

We do not sell users' personal information or personal audio recordings.

SingStrument does not upload personal song recordings to Firebase for advertising purposes.

13. Children's Privacy

SingStrument is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

14. Third-Party Services

SingStrument relies on certain third-party platform services, including Google Firebase and Google Play services, for authentication, security, protected backend functionality, and delivery of official app resources.

These third-party services may process limited technical or account information according to their own privacy policies.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, privacy practices, or legal requirements change.

When this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will also be updated.
