Account & Data Deletion

Application: SingStrument (com.singstrument)
Developer: Ammobe Studio
Last updated: October 13, 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your SingStrument account and the associated cloud account information.

This page is available even if you have uninstalled SingStrument or no longer have access to the app.

Option 1 — Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open SingStrument and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Find the Account section.
  4. Select Delete Account & Data.
  5. Review the confirmation and confirm deletion.

You may need to sign in again to verify that you own the account.

Option 2 — Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion through our support email.

Send your request to:
singlovestrument@gmail.com

Suggested subject:
SingStrument Account Deletion Request

Request Account Deletion

If the button does not open your email app, manually compose an email to the address above.

Account Ownership Verification

Where possible, send your request from the email address associated with your SingStrument account.

We may require additional verification to confirm ownership before permanently deleting an account. This protects users against unauthorized requests.

If you no longer have access to your registered email address, explain the situation in your request so we can advise you about available verification steps.

Never email us your password, Google verification code or private signing keys.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Once an authorized request is processed, deletion generally covers the account data associated with your SingStrument identity, including:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your related Firestore user profile.
  • Instrument requests linked to your account, where maintained in our backend.
  • Other personal cloud account information associated with the deleted account, except information legitimately retained as described below.

What May Be Retained?

Limited security, abuse-prevention, administrative audit or legally required records may need to be retained where a legitimate reason exists.

Where feasible, identifying details in retained operational records will be removed or minimized. Such records are not retained for unrestricted use.

For details, see our Privacy Policy.

What About Local Music Files?

SingStrument is designed to keep your personal audio recordings, detected notes, musical projects and imported SoundFonts on your device.

Cloud account deletion does not necessarily erase files that you separately exported into public folders on your phone.

You can remove exported WAV, MIDI and other files from your device using its file manager or other appropriate storage controls.

How Long Does Deletion Take?

In-app requests are processed through the application's configured deletion functionality, subject to successful verification and backend completion.

Email requests require manual review and ownership verification. We aim to process valid requests promptly and in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

We may contact you if further verification is necessary and will provide confirmation when the request has been completed, where practicable.

Important Information

Account deletion is intended to be permanent. Once completed, your deleted account and associated cloud profile information may not be recoverable.

If you later use SingStrument again, you may need to register or sign in as a new user.
